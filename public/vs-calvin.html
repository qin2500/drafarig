Five things to remember

  1. Mega Charizard Y, not Kangaskhan. Sun halves his Water. Heat Wave hits Mamoswine and Tsareena for 160–220% even through Light Screen.
  2. Staraptor that doesn't Mega on turn 1 and outspeeds Morpeko is Scarf Final Gambit (192 fixed damage). It KOs Charizard, Rotom-Fan, Morpeko. Ghosts are immune; Kangaskhan (212 HP) survives it.
  3. Aegislash walls Mega Staraptor. Close Combat does 0, Brave Bird 25–29% into Shield form. Its Close Combat does OHKO Kangaskhan, so keep Kanga away until Rap is down.
  4. Kill Volcarona first. In our sun its Overheat OHKOs Aegislash (even Shield) and Sinistcha. Morpeko outspeeds it: Fake Out or Taunt, then Weather Ball (81–96%).
  5. No Fake Out into Tsareena's side (Queenly Majesty). Staraptor's Intimidate puts Aegislash and Morpeko at −1; Scrappy Kangaskhan ignores it.
Focus Sash check. Mamoswine's and Jolteon's most common ladder item is Focus Sash. A Heat Wave or Weather Ball "OHKO" leaves them on 1 HP if they hold it. Spread chip, Ice Shard and Sash Counters matter.

Five ways we lose

  1. Final Gambit wipes Charizard T1. When Staraptor doesn't Mega, Protect Charizard or switch to Aegislash.
  2. Volcarona Quiver Dances behind Rage Powder and sweeps our Ghosts. Fake Out / Taunt it T1–T2; Heat Wave + Weather Ball.
  3. Mamoswine cleans up Charizard and Rotom-Fan with Rock Slide / Ice Shard. Wide Guard, Heat Wave Mamoswine first, keep Rotom away from it.
  4. Tailwind + screens snowball. Fake Out Grimmsnarl T1, go Trick Room, Protect through the 4 Tailwind turns.
  5. Rain for his Basculegion. Don't bring Pelipper; keep the sun up.

Endgame

  • Keep Aegislash for Mega Staraptor, our only full wall (Close Combat 0, Final Gambit 0).
  • Keep Charizard for Mamoswine, Tsareena, Basculegion. Never let it eat a Rock Slide: Wide Guard or Protect.
  • Morpeko's Sash is its life. Fake Out early; save the Sash for the Final Gambit or Close Combat turn.
  • Bad trades: Rotom into Mamoswine; Aegislash into Volcarona in sun; Kangaskhan into Rap while it's alive; Sinistcha into Volcarona or Basculegion after Coba is used.
  • Timing: sun 5 turns from the Mega turn. Tailwind 4 and Trick Room 5, both counting the turn set. Light Clay screens 8. Setting Trick Room while it's up cancels it.
