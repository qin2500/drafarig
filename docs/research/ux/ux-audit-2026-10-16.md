# UX audit: live v9 (prep-builder 67e226c)

Drafarig UI UX, 2026-10-16.

**Inputs**
- Screenshots in `/Users/anthony/.claude/jobs/e684a1f7/tmp/audit/` (00–11, at 1280 and 390, after importing Rotom-Fan and Charizard).
- The code on `prep-builder`. Each copy string quoted below is from `public/prep-copy.js` or `public/app.js`.

**How it's ranked:** by how much each problem hurts Anthony's real loop: pick an opponent → decide a 6 → tune sets → check numbers → play.

| Severity | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Blocker** | Stops the task, or makes him distrust the output. |
| **Major** | Costs real time or causes a wrong read. |
| **Minor** | Polish. |

The short version: **the numbers are good, but he can't see what they assume, can't get at them, and has to cross four screens to say "these are my 6".** Fixing #1–#3 matters more than everything below them combined.

## Blockers

### 1. Auto calcs are opaque, and you can't open them
- **Where:** editor move strip (`11-editor-move-strip-1280`), slider marks (`10-editor-*`), compare diff chips.
- **Problem:**
  - "Staraptor-Mega +2 161.9–191.5% KO" never says what Staraptor it assumes: spread, nature, item, ability.
  - The only hint is the strip header, "their common spread". The mark tooltip has one calc line and no inputs.
  - There's no way to change their SP, their item, the field or your boost to see what flips the result.
  - So the headline numbers can't be checked, and he says so.
- **Fix:** the calc workbench (`calc-workbench-teams.md` §B).
  - Every auto number becomes a button that opens a sheet with both full sets, sliders and the field, recalculating live, and closes back to where he was.
  - Also, today: put the assumed set on each strip card in one muted line, e.g. "Jolly 29/1/0/0/4/32 · Staraptite · ladder Sept".

### 2. You can't build "a team"; sets hang off single mons
- **Where:** My sets (`09-my-sets-*`), editor team strip (`10-editor-1280`), Sets tab (`06-sets-*`), Plan (`05-plan-1280`), Brief tray (`04-brief-1280`, bottom).
- **Problem:**
  - My sets is a list of 10 names with "+ Set" buttons.
  - The editor says **"YOUR 6: CHOOSE THEM ON A MATCHUP'S SETS TAB"**. That's an instruction to leave the screen, and it lands on a gate (#3).
  - There are three unrelated "6" pickers, which don't obviously share state:
    - the Brief tray "My 6 0/6" (species shortlist);
    - Plan "Your 6 · 0 of 6 picked" (species picks per plan card);
    - Sets "Your sets for this match" (`my6`, set ids).
  - Showdown players think in teams. This model fights that.
- **Fix:** Teams (`calc-workbench-teams.md` §C).
  - Sets live inside a team; a matchup *brings a team*; one picker everywhere.

### 3. Sets, Calcs and Practice are empty gates
- **Where:** `06-sets-1280`, `07-calcs-1280`, `08-practice-1280`.
- **Problem:**
  - Each tab shows only "Nothing is saved for Anthony vs Calvin yet. Starting saves prep on this device only." plus a **Start Anthony's prep vs Calvin** button.
  - It's a click and a decision just to *look*. The editor (#2) sends him here to pick a 6.
- **Fix:** no gate. Show the tab's content at once; the first edit creates the workspace silently (Plan already works this way: "your first pick saves it"). Keep the "saved on this device" line as a footer.

## Major

### 4. A wall of assumptions before any control
- **Where:** editor top (`10-editor-1280`: 3 lines; `10-editor-390`: 9 lines).
- **Problem:** "vs Calvin's 10 · their sets: saved, else ladder guesses (Smogon 2026-09, not this league) · their common spread unless stated · Lv 50 · single target (×0.75 when a move hits both) · no crits · Speed: their Lv 50 tiers (max +Spe, neutral max) or their saved set; Scarf, ladder spreads and Tailwind only when ticked · weather: none set by you (theirs shown per hit) · tap a mark for its calc". Nobody reads this, and on a phone it pushes the editor off screen.
- **Fix:** one row of 3–4 chips: `vs Calvin ▾` `ladder sets (Sept)` `Lv 50 doubles` `ⓘ`. ⓘ opens the full text. Per-number assumptions move into the workbench, which shows the actual inputs.

### 5. Slider marks are unreadable without hovering each one
- **Where:** `10-editor-*` (Spe row: 8 identical green bars; HP/SpD: dashed circles).
- **Problem:**
  - Unlabelled marks mean nothing at a glance, and on a phone there is no hover.
  - The "needs another nature/item" marks are dashed circles that **look like loading spinners**.
  - The legend is 11px text at the bottom. On the 390 view the slider is about 110px wide, so marks pile up ("●●●" at 0–3 SP on SpA).
- **Fix:**
  - (a) Label inline the **next mark above the thumb** and the **last one passed**, e.g. "next: outspeeds Mamoswine 132 at 27 · passed: Volcarona 120".
  - (b) Other-nature marks: hide them behind one "show marks for other natures/items" toggle, rather than drawing spinners.
  - (c) Phone: the stat name, number and final stat on one line, the slider full width beneath it, and taps of at least 44px. A tap opens the workbench at that value.

### 6. "Speed also counts" toggles are broken layout
- **Where:** `10-editor-1280` and `10-editor-390`, under the Spe row.
- **Problem:** four pill buttons ("+ Scarf users", "ladder spreads", "their Tailwind", "your Tailwind") stack vertically in about 60px of width, each word on its own line. It looks broken, and it adds about 280px of height.
- **Fix:** one horizontal chip row spanning the slider column, labelled "Count:". On a phone it wraps 2×2.

### 7. "No OHKO" chips contradict the strip next to them
- **Where:** `11-editor-move-strip-1280`.
- **Problem:** the SpA row says "Thunderbolt: no OHKO on Staraptor-Mega, Grimmsnarl, Basculegion-F at +0 even at 32". The strip above it says Basculegion-F **"93.3–110.7% 9/16 KO"**. "No OHKO" here means "not ≥ 15/16", which reads as false.
- **Fix:** "no *sure* OHKO even at 32 (best: 9/16 on Basculegion-F)", and tapping it opens the workbench.

### 8. The calc strip needs an instruction to work, and hides cards
- **Where:** `11-editor-move-strip-1280`.
- **Problem:**
  - The header ends with "· tap the move field again after changing it" (`TB.stripHead`): the strip doesn't follow a move change by itself.
  - Cards scroll sideways and the last one is cut ("Tsareen…").
  - The order is pool order, not relevance.
- **Fix:** update on change (no instruction); sort KOs and near-KOs first; wrap into a grid (2 columns on a phone); put the assumed set on each card (#1).

### 9. Deleting a set is instant, with no undo, and quietly edits matchups
- **Where:** `set-delete` (`prep-sets.js` L461).
- **Problem:** one tap on "Delete this set" (a bare button next to History) removes the set **and all its versions**, and silently drops it from every matchup's `my6`. There's no confirm and no Undo; the toast only says the calcs keep their inputs.
- **Fix:** an Undo toast for 10 s (the app already has `P.ui.undo` for other deletes), and the button inside a "⋯" menu. With Teams, delete becomes "remove from team", with the set kept in the box until it's emptied.

### 10. The same warning banner on every screen
- **Where:** all screenshots (orange: "1 name(s) couldn't be matched to a Pokémon…").
- **Problem:** it's a Draft-sheet typo (17's "Hisiuan Goodra"). In Battle Prep it costs 1 line on desktop and 4 on a phone on *every* screen, and it trains him to ignore orange.
- **Fix:** keep the banner in Draft/Setup. In Prep, show a small "⚠ 1" dot on the sync badge that opens Fix names. Allow "dismiss for this session".

### 11. Pasting a normal Showdown team imports nothing until you answer per set
- **Where:** `03-paste-check-1280`.
- **Problem:**
  - Any regular EV paste (252/252) needs **"Convert to Stat Points (suggested)"** tapped *per set*. Until then the primary button reads **"Import 0 sets"**.
  - The suggested answer is nearly always the right one.
  - Each set is also echoed line by line as a bulleted list, which is long and redundant.
- **Fix:**
  - Convert automatically when the conversion fits (it already computes "32 SpA / 32 Spe (2 left)").
  - Show one line per set ("Rotom-Fan: EVs 252/252 → SP 32 SpA / 32 Spe · undo"), and keep the question only for ambiguous cases.
  - Collapse the echo behind "show lines".

### 12. The Brief is a scroll marathon
- **Where:** `04-brief-1280` (4330px tall), `04-brief-390` (about 6000px).
- **Problem:**
  - Every "useful" mon card repeats "Why it looks useful / What it costs" chips, and every plan card repeats member lists.
  - The thing he needs on match day (lead ideas, the speed order for *his 6*) is several screens down.
- **Fix:**
  - Default to the "Your 6" lens once a team is chosen.
  - Show one line per card, expandable; cap each section at 3 items with "show all".
  - Move "Pairs to test" and "Next checks" into Plan.

### 13. The Speed summary is jargon
- **Where:** editor rank line (`TB.rank`): "Rotom-Fan Spe 138: outspeeds 6 of their 11 max-neutral tiers, 4 of their max-+Spe tiers". The diff bar has the same pattern (`TB.fasterThan`).
- **Fix:** "Spe 138: faster than 6 of Calvin's 11 if they run neutral max Speed, 4 if they run +Speed". Even better: "faster than 6 · slower than 5 (tap)", where the tap lists them.

### 14. Version vocabulary on a Showdown-style editor
- **Where:** editor title "Rotom-Fan · Set v1", and five buttons: Save version / Save as a new set / Discard / History / Delete.
- **Problem:** versions are an internal safety net, but they're the main vocabulary of the screen.
- **Fix:**
  - Title: the nickname or species, with the team as the breadcrumb.
  - **Save** keeps a version automatically; History sits under "⋯".
  - "Save as a new set" becomes "Duplicate set" (also under "⋯").

### 15. Three ways to pick an opponent on one screen
- **Where:** `01-prep-home-*`.
- **Problem:**
  - The top opponent strip, the "Your weeks" strip, the "Prep for a match" card grid and the "All 9 opponents…" select all do the same thing.
  - On a phone the grid is 9 tall cards (about 1500px) before Tools.
- **Fix:** keep the weeks strip (it's the schedule) and one compact grid. Drop the duplicate select on Overview.

## Minor

16. **"Is this you? Yes · Change"** sits at the top of every Prep screen until confirmed (`01`, `04`–`08`). It's correct behaviour, but it should be one first-run prompt and then gone. Today it reads like a permanent control.
17. **Draft after completion** (`00-draft-1280`):
    - The Budget panel says "Tight" with 0 pts / 0 slots.
    - The copy bug "points left minus the **-1** cheapest mons" (app.js L236, `slotsLeft - 1` when 0 slots are left).
    - Fix: when the roster is complete, replace Budget with the final roster card.
18. **Duplicate stat display** in the editor: the mini stat column (top right) repeats the final stats shown on every slider row. On desktop it's fine as Showdown flavour; on a phone it's already hidden. Keep it.
19. **Marks pop in late:** damage marks arrive after an idle job and show "Calculating…" per row, and the diff bar shows "Calculating…" with no progress. Fine once warmed up, but the first open of a mon looks unfinished. Show a single "Working out benchmarks…" line for the whole panel instead of per row.
20. **Item and move are free text with a datalist:** an unknown name toasts and reverts (good). But a near miss ("life orb ") toasts instead of matching. Trim and do a case-insensitive match before rejecting; partially done today.
21. **"Explain everything"** appears in Prep's top bar on desktop but changes little there. Hide it in Prep, or make it the ⓘ toggles (#4).
22. **Phone top bar:** the sync badge truncates to "14s ago ○", and Draft/Battle Prep plus the badge use 2 rows with the banner. Merge the badge into the brand row, as an icon only.
23. **Team strip on a phone** (`10-editor-390`) shows 2 of 10 mons with sideways scroll and no hint that more exist. Use a 5×2 grid of small sprites instead.
24. **"Ask the VGC agent about this screen"** sits at the very bottom of long pages (the Brief at about 6000px). Make it a floating button.

## What's working (keep it)
- Sprites everywhere, type colours, and the Plan board's Lead/Back slots: visual and Showdown-familiar.
- The Brief's speed track: the best single visual in the app.
- Benchmarks exist and are correct. They need to be **legible** (#5) and **openable** (#1), not reworked.
- Device-local saving, nothing sent anywhere: say it once, in the footer.
