# Sources — Drafarig UI/UX research

Gathered 2026-10-02. Notes say what each source added. "403" means the page blocked the fetch, so the note comes from search-result text only.

## Pokémon draft-league platforms and planners
- https://www.poketools.com/draft-assistant: PokeTools Draft League Assistant. Budget meter (spent, left, average per pick), 12 slots, a defensive matrix, STAB coverage counted out of 18 types, six role buckets, and the top 5 suggestions with a score and a one-line reason (for example "8 new super-effective targets"). The roster is kept in the URL so it can be shared. This is the closest existing tool to Drafarig.
- https://www.poketools.com/team-builder: PokeTools Team Builder. Checks threats against the top 20 by usage, flags "unchecked threats", gives a meta-conformity grade and lists proven cores.
- https://www.draftcentral.gg/: DraftCenter. A full league platform (snake and auction drafts, trades, standings) plus Team Lab (PokéPaste import, team sheet, pre-battle matchup check).
- https://www.draftcentral.gg/guides/what-is-pokemon-draft-league: plain-language explainer. Weekly opponent prep is "the heart of draft play".
- https://www.draftcentral.gg/formats: Champions Reg M-A (Apr 8–Jun 17 2026), M-B (Jun 17–Sep 8), and M-C (Sep 8–Dec 1 2026), which is current.
- https://draftdex.net/: DraftDex, a VGC-style platform. Live board with budget tracking, a Matchup Planner ("plan your Bring-6"), Bo3 scoring, and Reg M-A Mega support.
- https://indigo-draft.com/: Indigo Draft. Draft room with clock, pick queues, watchlists, tier-list import (CSV/TSV/JSON), matchup prep and "Lab what-if" workspaces, Showdown replay ingestion, and a Discord bot (Rotom-Draft).
- https://draftleague.net/: DraftLeague.net. Live board, timers, queued picks, auto-pick, team stats, speed tiers, weakness grids, Discord posts. (The page body did not load; features come from search snippets.)
- https://pokemondraftzone.com/ and https://pokemondraftzone.com/about: Pokémon DraftZone. Open source; matchup planner, speed chart with Scarf and Tailwind modifiers, drag-and-drop speed tiers, Markdown matchup notes, Champions M-A support. (Page body did not load.)
- https://www.smogon.com/forums/threads/pokemon-draftzone-the-ultimate-draft-league-website.3744321/: DraftZone launch thread (May 31 2024). Shareable matchup links and team comparison.
- https://www.onlinedraft.com/pokemon-draft/: OnlineDraft. Generic live board with a pick timer, snake order, and rules written "as plain sentences".
- https://fundrafted.com/guides/pokemon/team-analysis: Fundrafted. Separate defensive and offensive grids, a league-wide speed list with your roster highlighted, a warning about speed clumping, and in-draft updates of each grid after every pick.
- https://fundrafted.com/guides/pokemon/draft-league-guide: league-running guide. "Tight budget forces painful choices."
- https://fundrafted.com/guides/draft-strategy: snake seat strategy, stars-and-scrubs vs balanced, a 15–20 target queue, run management, and "always verify remaining budget covers minimum costs".
- https://www.randompokemon.co/draft-league-generator: pool generator for commissioners. Low relevance.
- https://monteams.com/: MonTeams planner. Red cells mark shared weaknesses, teal marks resists; "Coverage is 'hit the types that wall you.'"
- https://monteams.com/blog/pokemon-vgc-team-builder/: 6-step VGC framework (win condition, two-mode speed control, redirection, Fake Out/Intimidate, Protect on 4+ members).
