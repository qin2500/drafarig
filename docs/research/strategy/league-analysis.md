## 5. How Anthony should play this roster (beginner notes)

- **Default lead (Sun-Rain mode):** Kangaskhan (Fake Out) + Charizard (Mega Y). Back: Pelipper, Tinkaton/Sinistcha. Fake Out the biggest threat, Heat Wave. If they bring rain/Electric, swap in Sinistcha.
- **Tailwind mode:** Pelipper + Charizard-Y lead: Pelipper Tailwind (or Wide Guard vs Rock Slide leads), Charizard Heat Wave. Remember Pelipper's Drizzle will overwrite sun if it enters after Charizard; lead order decides the weather (the last setter to enter wins).
- **Trick Room mode:** Sinistcha + Kangaskhan (Fake Out) → Trick Room → slow partners (Tinkaton is mid-speed; Stunfisk-Galar / Sinistcha / Kingambit in Plan B benefit).
- **Mega choice per game:** Mega Y by default; Mega X vs teams with heavy Water/rain or Electric (Fire/Dragon resists both) or no Fairy/Dragon answers; Mega Kangaskhan only when Charizard is left on the bench.
- Use Protect a lot (only 8 PP in Champions, plenty for a game), double-target the opponent's Mega, and don't let Pelipper and Charizard sit on the field together into an Electric spammer.
